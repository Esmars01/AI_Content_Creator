byte-identical to the original bundles: only refs were normalized (bundles 0-7 had no tags; bundles 2-7

of the Phase 1 bundle) and the audit notes were added. Only the Phase 14 bundle gained commits: the
audit's corrections on top of 64816c4 (see docs/FINAL_AUDIT_AND_FIX_REPORT.md, section 18), and the
product-level audit of 2026-10-07 (docs/PRODUCT_LOGIC_AUDIT_REPORT.md: journeys, artifact checks, the
test matrix, 47 fixes and the GPU readiness decision).
